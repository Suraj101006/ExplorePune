# Explore Pune — Authoritative Source Bibliography & Archival Directory

## 1. Central Government & National Heritage Institutions

1. **Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) — Mumbai & Aurangabad Circles**
   - **Authority Scope:** Protection, conservation, ticketing, and monument administration for Centrally Protected Monuments of National Importance.
   - **Covered Destinations:** Shaniwar Wada, Aga Khan Palace, Shivneri Fort, Lohagad Fort, Karla Buddhist Caves, Bhaja Buddhist Caves, Pataleshwar Monolithic Cave Temple, Lenyadri Caves.
   - **Official Portal:** `https://asi.nic.in`
   - **Ticketing Engine:** Archaeological Survey of India E-Ticket Portal (`https://asi.payumoney.com`)
   - **Verification Standard:** Standard ₹25 Indian citizen / ₹300 foreigner admission fee schedule; dawn-to-dusk hours (06:00 AM - 06:00 PM).

2. **National Archives of India & Ministry of Culture**
   - **Authority Scope:** Archival records, treaties, and cartographic surveys of the Maratha Empire and British Residency at Poona.
   - **Covered Destinations:** Kesari Wada, Nana Wada, Lal Mahal, Purandar Fort (1665 Treaty of Purandar).
   - **Official Portal:** `https://nationalarchives.nic.in`

3. **Central Railway (Pune Division) & Railway Board**
   - **Authority Scope:** Administration, preservation, and public exhibition of steam locomotives and historic rolling stock at the Pune Railway Heritage Museum.
   - **Covered Destinations:** Pune Railway Heritage Museum (Near Pune Station).
   - **Official Portal:** `https://cr.indianrailways.gov.in`

## 2. Maharashtra State Government Authorities

1. **Maharashtra Tourism Development Corporation (MTDC)**
   - **Authority Scope:** State tourism promotion, authorized destination guides, trekking circuits, and regional tourist information bureaus.
   - **Covered Destinations:** All 106 destinations across Pune, Junnar, Bhor, Maval, Mulshi, and Haveli talukas.
   - **Official Portal:** `https://www.maharashtratourism.gov.in`

2. **Directorate of Archaeology & Museums, Government of Maharashtra**
   - **Authority Scope:** State protected monuments, rock-cut inscriptions, and district historical surveys.
   - **Covered Destinations:** Bedse Caves, Rajgad Fort, Torna Fort, Malhargad Fort, Tikona Fort, Visapur Fort, Rohida Fort, Bhor Rajwada.
   - **Official Portal:** `https://cultural.maharashtra.gov.in`

3. **Maharashtra Forest Department (Pune Division & Wildlife Circles)**
   - **Authority Scope:** Forest conservation, standing wildlife curfews, sanctuary guidelines, and hill fort access regulations.
   - **Covered Destinations:** Sinhagad Fort (Khadakwasla/Donje Range), Bhimashankar Wildlife Sanctuary, Tamhini Ghat Forest Reserve, Taljai Hills, Vetal Tekdi.
   - **Official Portal:** `https://mahaforest.gov.in`
   - **Field Notification Standards:** Standing curfew prohibiting visitor ascent past 06:00 PM; Joint Forest Management Committee (JFMC) toll collection at Golewadi Checkpoint.

4. **Tribal Research & Training Institute (TRTI), Government of Maharashtra**
   - **Authority Scope:** Ethnographic collections, tribal heritage galleries, and indigenous handicraft documentation.
   - **Covered Destinations:** Tribal Museum (Queen's Garden Road, Camp).
   - **Official Portal:** `https://trti.maharashtra.gov.in`

## 3. Local Municipal & District Administration

1. **Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC Heritage & Garden Departments)**
   - **Authority Scope:** Municipal Grade-I/II heritage structures, city wadas, public memorial halls, civic parks, and botanical reserves.
   - **Covered Destinations:** Lal Mahal, Vishrambaug Wada, Nana Wada, Mahatma Phule Wada, Balgandharva Ranga Mandir, Pu La Deshpande Garden, Saras Baug, Katraj Snake Park & Zoo, Pashan Lake.
   - **Official Portal:** `https://pmc.gov.in`
   - **Verification Standard:** Municipal resolution ticket fees (₹5 to ₹40); weekly maintenance closures (e.g. Zoo on Wednesdays).

2. **Pune District Administration (Collectorate & Disaster Management Authority)**
   - **Authority Scope:** Public safety alerts, monsoon waterfall prohibitory circulars under Section 144, ghat road landslide advisories.
   - **Covered Destinations:** Tamhini Ghat, Bushi Dam, Madhe Ghat, Mulshi Dam, Pavana Lake.
   - **Official Portal:** `https://pune.gov.in`

## 4. Public Transit & Mobility Feeds

1. **Pune Mahanagar Parivahan Mahamandal Ltd (PMPML)**
   - **Authority Scope:** Metropolitan bus route network, suburban timetables, bus terminal platforms, and fare schedules across PMC and PCMC limits.
   - **Covered Destinations:** All 106 destinations with authentic bus corridor numbers (e.g., Bus 50 for Sinhagad Paytha; Buses 9, 11, 24 for Shaniwar Wada & Mandai).
   - **Official Portal:** `https://pmpml.org`
   - **GTFS Source:** Pune Open Data Project & PMPML Scheduled Corridor Matrix (`data/pmpml-gtfs.json`).

2. **Maha Metro Pune (Pune Metro Rail Corporation)**
   - **Authority Scope:** Purple Line 1 (PCMC Bhavan ↔ Swargate) and Aqua Line 2 (Vanaz ↔ Ramwadi) station concourses, ticketing rules, and WhatsApp ticketing interfaces.
   - **Official Portal:** `https://punemetrorail.org`
   - **Key Intermodal Hubs:** Swargate Multimodal Concourse, Civil Court Interchange, Pune Railway Station, Mandai Station.

## 5. Academic Gazetteers & Curatorial Research Foundations

1. **Gazetteers of the Bombay Presidency: Poona District (Volume XVIII, Parts I, II, & III, 1885 / Revised 1954)**
   - **Authority Scope:** Foundational epigraphical, architectural, topographical, and genealogical records of Pune's forts, wadas, ancient shrines, and water management tanks.
   - **Digital Repository:** Maharashtra State Gazetteers Department (`https://gazetteers.maharashtra.gov.in`).

2. **Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute (BORI)**
   - **Authority Scope:** Sanskrit, Prakrit, and Persian manuscripts; Mahabharata critical edition research; oriental epigraphy.
   - **Official Portal:** `https://bori.org`

3. **Bharat Itihas Sanshodhak Mandal (BISM)**
   - **Authority Scope:** Primary Modi script bakhars, farmans, sanads, and correspondence of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, Sambhaji Maharaj, and the Peshwa court.
   - **Official Portal:** `https://bismpune.org`

4. **Temple Trusts & Devasthan Committees**
   - **Shreemant Dagdusheth Halwai Ganpati Trust:** `https://dagdushethganpati.com`
   - **Shree Kasba Ganpati Sarvajanik Ganeshotsav Mandal:** `https://kasbaganpati.org`
   - **Shree Kshetra Alandi Devasthan:** `https://alandidevasthan.org`
   - **Shree Kshetra Dehu Sansthan:** `https://dehutemple.org`
   - **Bhimashankar Jyotirlinga Devasthan Trust:** `https://bhimashankar.in`
   - **BAPS Swaminarayan Sanstha:** `https://baps.org`

## 6. Verification Protocol & Quality Standard

Every fact in the Explore Pune dataset is evaluated against this hierarchy:
1. **Tier 1 (Statutory Authority):** ASI, State Archaeology, Forest Dept, Municipal Gazetteers.
2. **Tier 2 (Operational Authority):** PMPML, Maha Metro, Registered Devasthan Trusts, Museum Directorates.
3. **Tier 3 (Academic & Historical Research):** Poona Gazetteers, BORI, BISM, University Department publications.
4. **Field Ground-Truth:** Direct GNSS coordinates recorded at physical visitor entrance wickets, parking gates, and suburban bus boarding platforms.
